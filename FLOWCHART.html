Trading Desk: how every agent works (paper trading only, NSE F&O stock options)

Updated 4 Nov 2026. Nothing here places real orders; INDmoney access is read-only. Rules from the user: buy options only (CALL for up, PUT for down), no stop-loss, square-off 15:20 except the overnight carry rule. Four teams trade side by side with their own paper money.

2. The four teams: how each one decides

PREOPEN team (Rs 1 lakh)

A. Gap recovery (new 4 Nov): stock opens 1.5%+ from yesterday's close

▼

Wait to 09:30: price back 1%+ from the early extreme AND past the open

▼

Skip: results day, or strong news behind the gap

▼

Buy the recovery direction (CALL after gap-down, PUT after gap-up)
B. Pre-open order flow and results-day gaps (gapfollow.py): follow the gap from 09:30 if the first 15 min agree
Backtest: recovery +Rs 33.6k on 35 trades; continuation loses.

NEWS team (Rs 1 lakh)

Idea: analyzer forecast, results filing, or quiet spurt the news agrees with

▼

Skip: stock's own results day (for forecasts), already priced in (Expectations)

▼

Price confirms the direction

▼

Trend Checker scores the chart 60+ (momentum.py)

▼

Buy CALL (up) or PUT (down)
Live record is negative so far; being reviewed.

SPURT team (Rs 1 lakh)

NSE volume 1.5x+ usual at a 0.2-0.5% move; candle and VWAP side agree (spurt.py)

▼

Live INDmoney quote still fits the move (0.1-0.8%)

▼

Skip: strong news against it. If news agrees, the trade goes to the news team

▼

F&O OI build-up noted; Probability Analyzer logs (observe)

▼

Buy CALL (up) / PUT (down); target 15% if Risk allows, else 10%
No chart-strength check today (tested 4 Nov: no filter added money).

MOVERS team (Rs 30,000)

Top gainers/losers of the day (INDmoney movers)

▼

Chart reader: reversal setup, support/resistance, regime (chart_reader.py, regime.py)

▼

News Guard: skip results, regulatory action, policy hit, strong news against (news_guard.py)

▼

Shock days: no fading gainers in a rally, no bounces in a crash

▼

Buy PUT on a fading gainer / CALL on a bouncing loser; 1% target

3. A trading day

Time (IST)What happens
08:40Morning brief: overnight filings, expected Nifty open (US stocks, crude), day mode, mover forecast; watcher started
09:08 / 09:10Pre-open snapshot saved; mover forecast message
09:15 - 15:15Entries. Preopen gap recovery decided at 09:30. NSE filings every 10 s; spurts every minute
12:40Midday trades report; journal pushed
13:40, 14:40Watcher health check (restart if the container killed it)
15:20Square-off or carry overnight
15:40Evening review and report
